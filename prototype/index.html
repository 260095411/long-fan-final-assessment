02

Three checks, three exchanges

Cases I met

A yes or no answer is the last step of a check, and the only step the visitor is shown. Everything before it — frames, a document image, a card, a call to a bank — is recorded elsewhere. When the answer is wrong, the person meets the same screen as a correct refusal.

03

Notes

Why these three. Ofcom lists seven methods as capable of being highly effective at 18: credit card checks, digital identity services, email-based age estimation, facial age estimation, mobile-network checks, open banking and photo-ID matching. Three are used here. Face scanning in the background, and “Face ID already knows your age”, are left out, because no public documentation shows them returning an age.

Where the hops come from. Each hop names the product it was copied from and quotes that product's own text — Yoti's selfie check as it runs inside Instagram, Yoti's document check, Steam's card check. What each source settles, and what it leaves open, is listed below.

The second arrangement. “With a proof” replaces the capture at the gate with a reusable claim held in a wallet or a bank app. The gate looks the same; what changes is who has to be trusted, and how much is left behind.
